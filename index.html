A little about me

Writing from the heart

I’m Mansoureh Aramfard, an author, abstract painter, and visual artist with a master’s degree in psychology and a bachelor’s degree in chemistry. I’m also interested in artificial intelligence and creativity. I write about human relationships, self-awareness, love, kindness, and inner peace.

Through my writing, I hope to offer a quiet moment of reflection, a little encouragement, and a reminder that every day can hold a fresh beginning.
