A little about me

Writing from the heart

I’m Mansoureh Aramfard, an author, abstract painter, and visual artist with a master’s degree in psychology and a bachelor’s degree in chemistry. I’m also interested in artificial intelligence and creativity. I write about human relationships, self-awareness, love, kindness, and inner peace.

Through my writing, I hope to offer a quiet moment of reflection, a little encouragement, and a reminder that every day can hold a fresh beginning.

Love Is the Freedom to Become New

Love and freedom from old images

An original bilingual reflection on seeing people as they are today and giving love, relationships, and ourselves the freedom to become new.

Read the full reflection

Sometimes we do not see people as they are today; we still look at them through the image of who they were yesterday, as though one behavior, one decision, or one chapter should define them forever.

Yet every human being is continually learning, understanding, and changing. Someone may have grown calmer, discovered a new perspective, or quietly rebuilt a part of themselves.

Love is more than remembering who someone has been; it is the art of seeing them again. It means listening before assuming what they will say, asking about who they are today before reaching a conclusion, and allowing their new truth to reveal itself.

When we keep someone confined to an old role, the conversation ends before it begins. But when we make room to notice how they have changed, the relationship begins to breathe again, and closeness takes on a more living meaning.

We deserve that same opportunity. We do not have to spend our lives proving that we are no longer the person we once were. We can renew the image we hold of ourselves and live from the understanding we have today.

Perhaps one of the purest forms of love is to say to another person—and to ourselves:

I will not keep you framed in yesterday. I want to know who you are today.

Rivers of Color

In this abstract painting, colors flow together like lively rivers. Turquoise and blue evoke the calm of water; green and yellow bring a sense of freshness and light, while touches of pink and magenta add warmth and energy. Each wave and pattern creates a new path, inviting the eye on a journey through color. This artwork is an invitation to pause, look closely, and discover a different story each time.
